# Brainstorming & prototyping — gripper barbatronic + hinge

Exploration of a **gripper ("barbatronic")** and **hinge (charnière)** mechanisms for grabbing and lifting crates.

## Contents
- `gripper barbatronic/` — gripper design (also as a `.rar` archive)
- `multiple-joint-hinges*`, `multiple joint hinge near final/`, `hinge images/` — multi-joint hinge study
- `free rotation mechanisme/`, `servo rotation mechanism + rail/` — free-rotation and servo-driven rotation mechanisms
- Ball-screw / hinge + gripper parts, crate stopper and crate upper holder, screw-and-nut motor holder and bearing supports
- `prototype/`, `piece util/` — prototype files and reusable parts

Part of the CAD work of the **Aerobotix (INSAT, Tunisia) Eurobot 2026 team** — see the [organization](https://github.com/Eurobot-2026-CAD-departement) for the other repositories.
